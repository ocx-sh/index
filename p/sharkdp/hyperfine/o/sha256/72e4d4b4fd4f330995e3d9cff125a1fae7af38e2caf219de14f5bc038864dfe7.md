# hyperfine

hyperfine is a command-line benchmarking tool. It runs commands repeatedly,
performs configurable warmup runs to fill caches, detects statistical outliers,
and reports the mean, standard deviation, min, and max of execution times. It
supports parameterized benchmarks, comparison of multiple commands with relative
speedups, and export of results to JSON, CSV, Markdown, and AsciiDoc.

## What's included

- **hyperfine** — the benchmarking CLI, with bundled shell-completion scripts
  and the `hyperfine.1` man page.
