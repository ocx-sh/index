# Privacy — what this site processes

Almost nothing. `index.ocx.sh` is a set of static files. There is no
account, no form, no analytics, no cookie, and nothing on any page is
loaded from another company's server. What remains is the request your
browser makes to fetch each page, which the host processes — this notice
exists to say so precisely.

technical data every web request carries: your IP address, the time, the

## No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookie and runs no analytics, tag manager, or any
advertising or tracking script. Nothing is fetched from a third-party
server while you read: the typefaces (IBM Plex) are bundled with the site
itself, and package logos are stored in this index's own content store
rather than hot-linked from a registry.

The site remembers display choices you make, in your browser's local
storage: `vitepress-theme-appearance` (light/dark), `ocx-catalog-view`
(cards/table) and `ocx-catalog-sort` (sort order). They stay on your
device, are never transmitted anywhere, and hold no identifier. Clearing
your browser's site data removes them.

## Links to other sites

Pages here link out to GitHub, container registries and upstream project
sites. Following such a link hands you to that operator, whose own privacy
notice then applies. Nothing is sent to them until you click.

(Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and you may object to processing
based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). Write to the address above.
